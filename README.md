## About

I take models from research into production. Right now that means **vision-language models that read invoices**: fine-tuning Qwen VLMs with LoRA and GRPO, serving them over vLLM behind FastAPI, and building the synthetic data that trains them. My M.Sc. thesis at Universidade de Lisboa is on the same problem: turning pixels into schema-valid structured output.

- 🧪 **Now** — ML Engineer at [Comudel](https://comudel.com); 27B VLM at ~0.96 field-level accuracy, shipped to staging
- 🎓 **M.Sc. Data Science**, FCUL · Universidade de Lisboa (2026)
- 🔬 **Before** — gait recognition at LASIGE · Neo4j healthcare chatbot at Object Automation · sign-language detection at Tact Labs
- 📍 Lisbon, Portugal · open to opportunities
